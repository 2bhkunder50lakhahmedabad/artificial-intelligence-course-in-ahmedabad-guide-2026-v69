Understanding Artificial Intelligence: The Basics

Before embarking on your journey through an Artificial Intelligence Course in Ahmedabad, it's essential to grasp what artificial intelligence entails. AI is the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, especially computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. AI systems can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, like visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.
